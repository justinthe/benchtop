# Why white light splits in a prism

Glass slows violet more than red, so a prism bends violet further and a narrow white beam opens into a fan.
I want people to rotate the prism and see the fan open and close, and find the minimum deviation angle where
the bend is smallest. Show what happens when the beam hits the exit face too steeply and gets trapped inside
(total internal reflection). Maybe a second prism that puts the colours back together into white.

Use dense flint glass (N-SF11). Put it on an optics bench in a sunlit room, with a screen where the fan lands.
